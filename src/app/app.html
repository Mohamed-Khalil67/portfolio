About

I started out in automotive embedded systems writing C, where I learned to respect clean, precise code and the cost of getting things wrong. I moved into web development to build things people can actually see and use - and found that the hardest, most rewarding part is the front end done properly. Today I focus on the difficult corners: CSS that stays maintainable at scale, layouts that adapt instead of just shrinking, and interactions that feel effortless. Angular and TypeScript on the front, NestJS and PostgreSQL behind it when a project needs the full stack.
